Route

A single closed walk covering every path in Green-Wood Cemetery at least once.

This route covers — miles across — turns, with —% of the distance spent retracing paths already walked and — feet of total climbing. Walking it in — hours means keeping a pace of — minutes per mile.

The path network is from Sevtsuk, Basu, Liu, Alhassan & Kollar (2025), “Spatial distribution of foot traffic in New York City and applications for urban planning”, Nature Cities — a citywide pedestrian network built from NYC's planimetric sidewalk data, corrected and hand-verified against aerial imagery for routing use, clipped here to Green-Wood Cemetery.

Elevation is from NYC's Office of Technology and Innovation, “NYC Planimetric Database: Elevation Points” — spot elevations captured at building footprint corners citywide. Each stop along the route is matched to its nearest such point.

This specific path is a solution for Guan's Route Problem (also known as the Chinese Postman Problem). It's expected to be the shortest possible walk that covers every path.
